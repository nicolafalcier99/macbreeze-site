⚠️ Important: update as soon as possible

  • Fans stay at full speed for the whole of a temperature safety trip. The fix in 1.0.21 missed a case: if the fan helper restarted while the Mac was already hot, a fan could drop back to automatic and stay there.

Fixed

  • Blast stays on until you turn it off. Before, the fans could slow down after a safety trip while the button still showed Blast Engaged.
  • A fan curve can no longer overwrite Blast on one fan just after you press it.
  • Fans in Sensor mode come back down when you turn Blast off. Before, they could stay at full speed until the temperature changed.
  • MacBreeze no longer hides the update window when it hides its own window.
  • Install and Relaunch works with Settings or another panel open. This takes effect from the update after this one.
  • MacBreeze no longer hides its window when an update starts downloading in the background.
