New

  • Safety now lists your recent Auto-Blast trips: when each one happened, which sensor caused it and how long it lasted. The list stays on your Mac. If you want help, you can open it as an email draft to support and send it yourself.
  • Preferences has a Slow-down speed setting for Pro: how quickly fans come down in Sensor mode and after Blast. Speeding up is always instant.

Improved

  • When you quit MacBreeze, your fans go back to macOS within a few seconds. Auto-Blast runs only while MacBreeze is open.
  • Fans stay at full speed when MacBreeze updates during a safety trip.

Fixed

  • The main window opens when MacBreeze started in the background. Before, the button could do nothing.
  • MacBreeze no longer tries to quit itself in the background when no window is showing.
