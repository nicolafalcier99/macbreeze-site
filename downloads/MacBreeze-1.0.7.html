Fixed

  • Fan controls looked frozen in the menu bar. While an app automation is running, MacBreeze locks the fan settings so the rule stays in charge — but the menu bar never said so. It now tells you which app is in control, and gives you a link straight to App Automations to change it.
  • The trial-ended notice could be missed. MacBreeze only checked your licence when it started, so if your trial ended while it was running you weren't told until you next restarted it. It now checks once a day.

Privacy

MacBreeze now records the day your Mac last checked its licence, and whether it's on a trial, the free version, or a paid licence, so we can count how many people use it. Nothing about how you use the app is recorded, and the record deletes itself after 90 days without a check-in. The details are in the privacy policy at macbreeze.app.
