• Clicking the menu bar icon no longer drags the MacBreeze window in front of whatever you were working in.
  • Each fan's real speed now stays on screen while an app automation is running, instead of disappearing exactly when you want to check it.
  • Changing a rule between "While Running" and "While Frontmost" now takes effect immediately, rather than waiting for that app to quit.
  • New switch on the App Automations page: whether the app you are working in takes the fans from a rule higher in the list, or the list order decides everything.
  • An open fan curve stays open — when you visit another page, and when you quit and come back.
  • The dashboard window now keeps one size, and Preferences can open it centred on screen or put it away when you switch desktop.
  • This window now lists every version you missed, not only the newest one, and opens in the middle of the screen.
  • The dashboard dims behind this window, and the current-temperature dot on the fan curve is no longer hidden by its own dashed line.
