• Fixed: battery temperature was missing on some Macs. MacBreeze was reading it from the wrong place in macOS and found nothing; it now reads it from where the battery actually reports it.
  • Fixed: after an update, the fan helper could keep running its older code until you restarted your Mac. MacBreeze now notices when the helper is behind and restarts it, so fixes to it take effect straight away.
  • The menu bar no longer shifts sideways while MacBreeze works out your battery's charge or drain rate. The long "Calculating…" is now a small hourglass that takes the same space as the reading, so the icons next to MacBreeze stay put.
  • Right after you plug in the charger, MacBreeze shows the hourglass instead of the plug. macOS can take half a minute or more to start charging, and the plug made it look as if the Mac wouldn't charge.
  • Fixed: the battery hourglass could stay up forever at ordinary drain rates and never settle on a reading.
