New

On macOS 27, the menu bar item can be used in one movement, the way a menu is: press, slide, let go.

  • Press the menu bar item, keep the button down, slide to Blast, Open Dashboard or Quit, and let go. The button under the pointer is ringed in blue, and it fires when you let go.
  • To change profile the same way, rest on the profile picker for a moment. Your profiles appear under it: slide to one and let go.
  • Or press and hold still on the menu bar item for half a second. A short list takes the panel's place: Blast, your profiles, Open Dashboard and Quit.

Improved

  • Right click on the menu bar item now lists your profiles too.
