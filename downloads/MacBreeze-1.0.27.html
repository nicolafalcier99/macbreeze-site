Improved

  • The menu bar panel now sits flat under the menu bar, like the Mac's own Wi-Fi and Battery panels: no arrow, and the system's see-through background in place of the solid one.
  • Press Esc to close the panel. It also closes when you switch desktop.
  • Opening the panel no longer takes you away from the app you are working in.

Fixed

  • After an Auto-Blast safety trip, fans on a curve or a Manual speed ease down to that speed and stay there. Before, they eased all the way down to their minimum and were handed back to macOS, so for about half a minute they could run far below your curve. Under a steady heavy load that let the Mac heat up again and trip Auto-Blast again, over and over.
