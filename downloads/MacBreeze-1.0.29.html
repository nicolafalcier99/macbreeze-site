Improved

  • A held press on the menu bar item now does one thing. The full panel stays open: slide to a button or a profile and let go. The short list that took the panel's place after half a second is gone, because two different things on one long press was one too many.

Fixed

  • An app automation added while its app is already open now takes over at once. Before, it did nothing until that app was quit and opened again: no app icon in the menu bar, and your profile still shown as the one in charge.
  • MacBreeze could quit by itself on macOS 27 after running for many hours. The cause was in how the menu bar item was drawn.
  • A sensor reporting a nonsense value can no longer stop the app or the fan helper.
