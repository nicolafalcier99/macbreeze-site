Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

What data do we collect?

To create and manage your MacBreeze license, we collect your email address and a device identifier for the Mac your license is activated on. The device identifier is never stored in its original form — it's cryptographically hashed before it reaches our servers, so we can't reconstruct your Mac's actual hardware ID. When MacBreeze checks its licence — when it starts, and once a day while it runs — we also record the date of that check and whether that Mac is on the free trial, the free version, or a paid licence. We keep only the most recent date, and delete it automatically once a Mac hasn't checked in for 90 days. Beyond that, we collect nothing about how you use MacBreeze or your Mac.

How do we use this data?

Your hashed device identifier is used to start your 14-day free trial and to enforce the per-license device limit (1 Mac for Pro, up to 4 for the 4-Pack) described in our FAQ, and to count how many Macs used MacBreeze in a given week or month, split into trial, free and paid. We only ever look at these as totals. Your email is used only to deliver your license key and support responses. We don't share this data with any third party beyond the payment and email-delivery processors below, and we don't use it for marketing.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects and handles your payment details directly — MacBreeze never sees or stores your card information. See Stripe's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

License delivery

After purchase, your license key is emailed to you via Resend, our email delivery provider. Resend processes your email address only to deliver that message. See Resend's privacy policy for details.

Updates to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
