Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

What data do we collect?

To create and manage your MacBreeze account and license, we collect your email address and a device identifier for the Mac your license is activated on. That's it — MacBreeze doesn't collect usage telemetry, crash reports, or any data about how you use your Mac.

How do we use this data?

Your email and device identifier are used only to validate your license and enforce the one-Mac-at-a-time limit described in our FAQ. We don't share this data with any third party, and we don't use it for marketing or analytics.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects and handles your payment details directly — MacBreeze never sees or stores your card information. See Stripe's privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

Updates to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
