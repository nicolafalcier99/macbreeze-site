Set it once. It runs itself.

Pick a fan profile for any app — Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Photoshop, or whatever else tends to run hot. The moment that app launches, MacBreeze switches to its profile automatically, and switches back the moment you quit. No manual toggling, nothing to remember.

Free includes 1 active app automation. Pro unlocks unlimited automations, with priority ordering when more than one could apply.
